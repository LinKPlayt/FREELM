Save and post

  1. Tap Save / Share on a reel.
  2. Choose Save Video to put it in Photos, or pick YouTube, TikTok or Instagram to post it.
  3. Tap Copy title and caption and paste it into your post. It includes the photo credits.

Sound test

Makes a 3-second test video with three notes, then checks that the sound made it into the file. If a reel ever comes out silent, run this and send us the results.

Install on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Privacy

  • Videos are made and stored on this phone only. Nothing is rendered or kept on a server.
  • The app looks up public information while it works: news feeds, Wikipedia, and free photo libraries (Openverse, Wikimedia Commons).
  • No account, sign-in or tracking.
